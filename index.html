Data sources
U.S. Drought Monitor
NDMC / USDA / NOAA
droughtmonitor.unl.edu
Weekly D0–D4 drought classification. Updated every Thursday.
Fire perimeters (YTD)
WFIGS / NIFC
data-nifc.opendata.arcgis.com
Current-year wildfire perimeters updated continuously during fire season.
Red flag warnings
NOAA / National Weather Service
api.weather.gov
Active fire weather watches and red flag warnings from NWS forecast zones.
Live weather conditions
Open-Meteo
open-meteo.com
Current temperature, humidity, wind speed and gusts. Free, no API key required.
Geocoding & Basemap
Google Maps Platform
developers.google.com/maps
Address geocoding, reverse geocoding, Places Autocomplete, and satellite/road basemap tiles.
NASA FIRMS hotspots
NASA EOSDIS / GIBS
firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov
VIIRS 375m thermal anomaly detections from NOAA-20 and Suomi NPP satellites. Updated every ~12 hours.
NIFC 7-Day Fire Potential
National Interagency Coordination Center
fsapps.nwcg.gov
Daily 7-day significant fire potential forecast. Normal / Elevated / Above Normal / Critical.
ISO fire protection class
ISO / Verisk (estimated)
isomitigation.com
Estimated PPC based on urbanization and state. Verify with ISO directly for binding decisions.
Terrain & elevation
Google Maps Elevation API
developers.google.com/maps
5-point upwind elevation sample per assessment. Slope calculated per Rothermel fire spread model — upslope terrain increases fire rate of spread up to 4x.
Wildfire smoke (PM2.5)
EPA AirNow / USFS
fire.airnow.gov
Hourly PM2.5 air quality index from monitoring stations and PurpleAir sensors. Smoke damage exposure indicator for insured properties.
